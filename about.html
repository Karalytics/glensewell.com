My Approach

A practical, proven way to turn strategy into value.

01

Start With
the Business

I start with the business problem, the decisions that need to improve and the outcomes the organization is trying to achieve. Strategy is most useful when it is grounded in real use cases and business priorities.

Produces → Business Priorities · Use Cases · Success Measures
02

Understand
Where You Are

Before defining the future, I look at the current state — capabilities, processes, data, technology, people and governance. Understanding what already exists helps identify the gaps that actually need to be addressed.

Produces → Current-State Assessment · Capability Gaps · Maturity View
03

Design the
Future State

I translate those priorities and gaps into a practical target state: the capabilities, operating model, products, architecture and governance required to support the organization's objectives.

Produces → Target Operating Model · Capability Model · Architecture · Product / Solution Design · Governance
04

Turn Strategy Into
Execution

A strategy only creates value when it can be implemented. I focus on prioritization, sequencing, roadmaps, ownership and measurable outcomes so organizations have a practical path from where they are today to where they want to be.

Produces → Roadmap · Prioritized Initiatives · Implementation Plan · Ownership · KPIs & Outcomes
